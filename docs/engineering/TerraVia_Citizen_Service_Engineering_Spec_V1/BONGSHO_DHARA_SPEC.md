# TerraVia Citizen Service — Bongsho Dhara Specification V1

## Purpose
Preserve family relationships, memories, archives and living testimony without converting family history into official identity truth.

## Domains
FamilyNetwork, Branch, Group, Relationship, Event, Calendar, Archive, LivingMemory, Research, Governance.

## Evidence levels
Officially Verified; Strongly Verified; Family Supported; Living Memory; Historical Oral Tradition; Unconfirmed Memory; Unresolved/Conflicting.

## Privacy
Relationship does not grant access. Living persons have privacy controls. Event audiences are explicit. Cross-family connections do not create broadcast rights.

## Living Memory
Preserve original recording, transcript, speaker, recording date, mentioned people/places, approximate dates, certainty and family review state. AI transcription may assist but cannot invent facts.

## Archive
Original files and metadata are preserved; versions are retained; historical uncertainty is explicit; export must be supported.

## Family governance
Roles: Member, Branch Coordinator, Archive Contributor, Family Moderator, Family Custodian, Family Knowledge Keeper. Roles provide workflow permissions, not truth authority.
