# TerraVia Citizen Service — Product & Business Model V1

## Product layers
### Citizen core
Identity, credentials, evidence, consent, readiness and secure transfer.
### Family premium
Bongsho, archive, Living Memory, family calendar, research and long-term storage.
### Organization network
Verification APIs, service contracts, credential verification, employment/education/professional workflows.
### Enterprise/public-sector interoperability
Adapter development, managed integration, compliance/audit tooling and service orchestration.
### Legacy
Long-term vault, succession and preservation services.

## Revenue opportunities
- Freemium citizen account with limited storage/advanced workflows.
- Premium family archive and Living Memory plans.
- Organization verification/API subscriptions.
- Integration/setup fees.
- Enterprise orchestration contracts.
- Managed adapter services.
- Long-term archival plans.

## Commercial rule
Do not monetize sensitive data by selling citizen profiles. Revenue should come from software, storage, workflow, integration, verification and service value.

## Strategic moat
Identity + Evidence + Consent + Policy + Interoperability + Life Events + Family Heritage + Legacy + Audit.
