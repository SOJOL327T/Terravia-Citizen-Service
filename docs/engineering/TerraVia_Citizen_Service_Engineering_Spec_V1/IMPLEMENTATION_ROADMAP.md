# TerraVia Citizen Service — Implementation Roadmap V1

## Stage 0 — Governance foundation
Freeze architecture, threat model, data classification, policy vocabulary, audit requirements and decision register.

## Stage 1 — Identity core
Person, identifiers, authentication, credential references, evidence and provenance.

## Stage 2 — Trust transaction core
Consent, authorization, policy engine, data request, data package, transfer, receipt and audit.

## Stage 3 — Service utility
Service catalog, application requirements, readiness engine, adapter SDK and first low-risk integrations.

## Stage 4 — AI assistance
Explain/prepare/recommend only. Add provenance, uncertainty labels, human confirmation and policy enforcement.

## Stage 5 — Family differentiation
Bongsho, relationships, calendar, archive, Living Memory and family governance.

## Stage 6 — Trusted Service Network
Organization identity, representatives, university/employer/professional verification and service contracts.

## Stage 7 — Legacy
Legacy Vault, custodians, succession and durable archival/export.

## Stage 8 — Civic network
Community memberships, civic issues, volunteer coordination and local emergency communication.

## Release gates
No stage advances until security, privacy, domain invariants, migration, audit and recovery tests pass.
