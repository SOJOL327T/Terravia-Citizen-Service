# TerraVia Citizen Service — Legacy Vault & Succession Specification V1

## Purpose
Provide controlled stewardship and generational continuity for user-selected memories, knowledge, documents and instructions.

## Legacy item
Content reference, owner, classification, visibility, release policy, custodians, backup custodians, trigger, retention, version history.

## Custodian principle
Custodian is a steward, not owner of the person's identity.

## Triggers
Verified death event, legally recognized trigger, scheduled date or other explicitly authorized trigger. Inactivity alone is never a death trigger.

## High-risk release
Use two-key or equivalent multi-party authorization for highly sensitive releases. Every release is audited.

## Modes
Private until trigger; time-locked; custodian-controlled; family-release; public-release where explicitly chosen.

## Recovery
Legacy vault requires durable backup, cryptographic key recovery planning, export and succession continuity. The system must be designed for long-term archival survivability.
