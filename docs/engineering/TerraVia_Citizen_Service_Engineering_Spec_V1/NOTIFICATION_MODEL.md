# TerraVia Citizen Service — Notification Model V1

## Notification classes
Security, Consent, Transfer, Application, Conflict, Life Event, Family Event, Legacy, Community.

## Rules
Notifications must be purpose-specific, configurable and privacy-preserving. Avoid revealing sensitive content in lock-screen previews by default.

## Critical notifications
Security compromise, high-risk transfer, consent change, identity conflict and legacy release require strong delivery and acknowledgement where appropriate.

## Quiet/fallback delivery
Support in-app plus configured email/SMS/push channels where lawful and available. Delivery status is auditable for high-risk notifications.
